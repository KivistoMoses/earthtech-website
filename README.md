EarthTech — ICT, Telecoms & Electrical Engineering
A clean, responsive landing page for EarthTech, an engineering firm based in Limbe, Cameroon. EarthTech delivers professional solutions across electrical power distribution, IT infrastructure, and telecommunications for industrial, commercial, and residential clients nationwide.
Features
Hero Banner: Modern full-width layout featuring core company focus and fast contact routing.
Service Categories: Structured 3-column breakdown covering:
Electrical Engineering: Industrial and domestic wiring, panel installations, power management, and safety audits.
IT & Hardware Support: System administration, workstation setup, hardware troubleshooting, and security surveillance systems.
Telecoms Infrastructure: Network design, fiber optic/Ethernet cabling, intercom setups, and signal routing.
Coverage & Deployment: Highlights local operations in Limbe alongside out-station capabilities for projects in Douala, Yaoundé, Bamenda, and across Cameroon.
Project Showcase: Responsive visual grid highlighting recent field installations.
Direct Client Channels: Integrated options for direct phone calls, WhatsApp consultations, corporate email, and Facebook.
